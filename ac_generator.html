Physics & observations

Φ = NBA cos θ
ε = NBAω sin θ

Here θ is the angle between the coil’s area normal and the magnetic field. The ideal generator is driven at a selected constant angular speed. Output current is ε/(Rload + Rinternal) when connected. The electrical load creates an opposing reaction torque; the external mechanical drive supplies the required energy. Idealized dimensions and uniform gap-field approximation.
