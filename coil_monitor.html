EXPERIMENT 06 · SIMPLE MOTOR

Motor experiment controls

Operate the motor

Starts automatically with the switch ON. Restart cycle returns the coil to the first turning point.

Electrical parameters

Continuous electrical contact

Both axle contacts are fully conducting. Conventional current continues around the complete circuit whenever the switch is closed, regardless of coil angle or rotation direction. No split-ring commutator is installed.

3D apparatus & visualization

Physics & observations

τ = N I A B sin α
N = 18 turns · A = 0.012 m²
B = 0.18 T · I = 0.60 A when connected
τmax = 23.3 mN·m

Model: rotating coil with two continuously conducting axle contacts in a nearly uniform field between two facing bar magnets. When the switch is closed, the idealized current I = V/R is present at every rotor angle. The magnetic torque τ = N I A B sin α changes sign as the coil passes through its equilibrium position. Without a split-ring commutator or electronic switching this is NOT a continuously running DC motor: the coil can reverse, oscillate, and settle. In this deliberately frictionless, idealized setup, the coil starts from rest at a turning point and automatically oscillates through a 180° arc. Changing the current or field changes the oscillation speed, not its turning-point angles. No commutator is installed.

Demonstration of the magnetic couple on a current-carrying rotating coil, without commutation.
