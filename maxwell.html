B = 10.0 μT

Maxwell's right-handed corkscrew rule: rotate the screw so it advances with the conventional current. The rotation gives the magnetic field direction. The displayed field assumes a long straight wire; connecting leads are not included in the field calculation. The screw is an analogy, not a part of the electrical circuit.

The direction is described from directly above the conductor; with current up, the field is anticlockwise.
